SELF-CARE / FIRST AID

First Aid Guide

Simple steps for minor health problems, and the signs that mean you should get medical help.

MINOR CONDITIONS
Emergency? Follow the mission emergency procedure and contact the medical team straight away for: loss of consciousness, severe bleeding, chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe allergic reaction, seizure, signs of stroke, or a head injury with confusion. Do not search this guide first.

First aid basics

1 · Stay safeCheck the area is safe for you and the person before helping.
2 · Stay calmSlow breathing helps you think clearly and reassures the patient.
3 · Keep it cleanWash hands or wear gloves. Use clean, sterile supplies.
4 · Ask for adviceIf you are unsure, contact the mission medical officer or Earth medical team.

This guide gives general first aid for minor problems. It does not diagnose illness or replace advice from the medical team. If symptoms get worse or you are worried, ask for medical help.
