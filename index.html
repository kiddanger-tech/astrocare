OFFLINE-READY SAFETY SUPPORT

First Aid & Communication Backup

A readily available reference for periods when a person cannot reach their healthcare team or mission control.

EDUCATIONAL PROTOTYPE
Urgent situation? Contact local emergency services immediately when possible. Follow instructions from a qualified emergency dispatcher or trained responder. This page is not a substitute for emergency services, certified first-aid training, or mission-approved procedures.

When communication is interrupted

Keep essential, professionally reviewed guidance available on the device. Record the time, symptoms reported, available measurements and actions taken. Retry the approved communication channel at the intervals defined by the responsible organisation.

Quick safety checklist

Cached reference
1. Check that the surroundings are safe before approaching.Safety
2. Seek emergency assistance or follow the approved mission escalation procedure.Escalation
3. Follow dispatcher instructions or the person's certified training.Guidance
4. Record observations and measurements with their source and timestamp.Record
5. Synchronise the record when a secure connection returns.Sync later
